WHO WE ARE

A Trading Company Focused on Medical Products

Stetig Limited is engaged in the wholesale and trading of medical supplies. We source and supply selected products from established manufacturers for customers and markets in Southeast Asia.

Our experience is concentrated in medical devices, particularly orthopedic implants, as well as regenerative and biomaterial products. Our role is commercial: sourcing, trading and supplying products.

A trade house, not a factory.
Stetig selects and trades products from established manufacturers. We do not design or manufacture the medical devices we supply.
